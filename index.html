01 Doctoral research

PhD in Computer Science

A doctoral thesis on characterizing users of enterprise social networks without reading message content or personal identifiers, grounded in fuzzy set theory and evaluated on real platform metadata at Beekeeper AG.

PhD thesis in Computer Science · University of Fribourg

Privacy-Compliant User Characterization in Enterprise Social Networks

The thesis designs, implements, and evaluates a framework that turns raw interaction metadata into fuzzy behavioral representations and groups users into overlapping, interpretable segments. Privacy is handled by design through consent handling, pseudonymization, and generalized attributes.

The work connects the Beekeeper industry case studies (ICEDEG 2024, Knowledge 2022) with a reusable open-source Python package and an SSRN framework paper.

Framework pipeline

  1. Data complianceConsent, pseudonyms, generalized bins
  2. Characterized user vectorAPI-event indicators and heuristic attributes
  3. Fuzzification engineMembership functions, alpha-cuts, rule layer
  4. Fuzzy clusteringFCM, PFCM, and Gustafson–Kessel vs. crisp baselines
  5. EvaluationStability, interpretability, and privacy metrics
